Type it. Likho writes it in your handwriting.

Teach Likho your hand once with a single printed sheet. After that, paste any text and get pages back in your own writing, ready as a PDF. It all happens on your laptop.

How it works

  1. Print the template and fill it in Two A4 pages, with each character written three times in the pen you normally use.
  2. Take a photo of each page Likho reads them in seconds and shows you every letter it learned. Redo any you don't like.
  3. Paste your text and export Preview as much as you want, then save the pages as a PDF.

What it can write

  • Questions, headings, underlines and lists
  • Maths: fractions, roots, sums, integrals, Greek letters
  • Code, with its line breaks and indentation kept
  • Tables, and simple diagrams like graphs and trees
  • The odd crossed-out word, the way a real hand does
