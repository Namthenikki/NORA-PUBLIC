# Handwriting template

[`likho-handwriting-template.pdf`](likho-handwriting-template.pdf) has 2 A4
pages and 128 characters: every keyboard character, plus the maths and code
symbols that come up most (→ ≤ ≥ ≠ × ÷ ± ∑ ∫ √ ∞ ∴ ∵ ∈ ∩ ∪ ∅ α β θ λ μ π σ Δ ∂
and more). You write each one three times.

## Printing

- A4 paper, at 100% / "actual size". Do not use "fit to page".
- Colour or black-and-white both work. A print shop page is fine.

## Filling it in

- Use the pen you normally write with: a dark blue or black ballpoint or gel
  pen. Don't use green, pencil or a highlighter.
- Write each character once per box, sitting on the line in the box.
- Write the way you normally do, not your neatest. Likho copies whatever you put
  down, and three slightly different copies make the result look natural.
- If a box goes wrong, leave it. Likho will ask you to redo that character.

## Photographing it

- Lay the page flat, in daylight or bright room light, with no shadow of your
  phone across it.
- Get **all four black corner squares** into the photo. Likho uses them to
  straighten the page.
- Hold the phone straight above the page, and take one photo per page.

Then open Likho and choose **Teach my handwriting**.
