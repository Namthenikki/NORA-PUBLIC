# Likho user guide

## 1. Install

Download the latest `Likho-Setup.exe` from the [Releases](../../../releases)
page and run it. Likho needs Windows 10 or 11 and no special hardware.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Choose
**More info → Run anyway**. This happens with new apps that haven't built up a
reputation with Microsoft yet.

## 2. Sign in

Sign in with your Google account. This is only used to keep track of your
pages: the 10 free ones and any you buy. Your handwriting and your documents
stay on your laptop.

## 3. Teach Likho your handwriting (free)

1. Print the [template](../template/) and fill it in. The template page
   explains how to print it, fill it and photograph it.
2. Copy the two photos to your laptop and choose **Teach my handwriting**.
3. Likho reads the pages in a few seconds and shows your alphabet. Check every
   character. If one came out wrong (cut off, smudged, or a neighbour's stroke
   got in), mark it and Likho will ask you to write just that one again.

You can redo the whole template any time, for example with a different pen.

## 4. Write

Type or paste your text into the editor. Likho understands a simple format for
headings, underlines, lists, code, maths and diagrams; see
[Writing format](writing-format.md). The preview updates as you type and is
always free.

## 5. Export

**Export PDF** writes the final pages. Each exported page uses one credit. Your
first 10 pages are free, and after that credits cost ₹2 per page, bought in
packs from **Buy pages** (UPI, cards and netbanking via Razorpay).

Preview pages carry a Likho watermark; exported pages don't.

## Tips

- Paste text, not screenshots. Likho writes text; it does not read images.
- Long code lines are written smaller to fit the page. Break them at about 40
  characters, as you would on paper.
- Likho adds the odd crossed-out false start, the way a real hand does. You can
  turn it off in the settings or the meta block.
