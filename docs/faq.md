# FAQ

**Does my handwriting get uploaded anywhere?**
No. Learning your hand and writing your pages both happen on your laptop. The
only thing Likho sends online is your page balance: who you are (your Google
account) and how many pages you have. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

**Do I need a good laptop or a graphics card?**
No. Any Windows 10/11 laptop works. Likho doesn't train a neural network; it
cuts your letters out of the template photos, which takes a few seconds.

**Do I need internet?**
Only to sign in and to get page credits. Previewing works offline, and you can
export as long as you have credits on that laptop.

**What counts as a page?**
One exported PDF page uses one credit. Previews are free and unlimited.

**Can I use Likho on two laptops?**
Yes. Sign in on both. Credits are tied to your account. Pages already fetched
to one laptop stay on that laptop.

**Can I make it write in someone else's handwriting?**
No. Likho is for your own handwriting only. Using someone else's handwriting or
signature is forgery and is against the [Terms](terms.md).

**My letters look broken or cut off.**
Redo those characters from the alphabet screen. The usual causes are a light
pen, a shadow across the page, or a corner square missing from the photo.

**Is Likho allowed for my coursework?**
That's up to your institution. Check its rules before you hand in anything
made with Likho.
