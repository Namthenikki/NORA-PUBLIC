# Privacy

Likho is built so that your handwriting never leaves your laptop.

## What stays on your laptop

- The photos of your template, and the letters Likho learns from them
- Everything you type into Likho, and every page it writes

None of this is uploaded, by Likho or by us.

## What the page-credit service stores

To keep track of your free and paid pages, a small online service stores:

- your Google account ID and email address (from Google sign-in);
- a one-way code for each laptop you use, so free pages can't be claimed
  twice on the same machine. This code can't be turned back into anything
  about your laptop;
- your page balance, and a record of each batch of pages sent to your laptop;
- for purchases: the Razorpay payment reference, amount and date. Card, UPI
  and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach us.

We use this only to run your page balance. We don't sell it, share it, or use
it for advertising.

## Deleting your data

Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove everything listed above,
except purchase records we're required to keep for tax purposes. Contact
details are on the Likho website.

## Children

Likho is not intended for anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's
consent.
